# PDPL Compliance Copilot

An AI-powered project designed to support privacy policy analysis against Saudi Arabia's Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL).

## Project Overview

PDPL Compliance Copilot is the final project of the AI Engineering Bootcamp. It explores the use of Artificial Intelligence (AI), Natural Language Processing (NLP), and Machine Learning (ML) to support personal data protection compliance.

The project combines legal information retrieval with privacy policy clause classification to help users identify relevant legal provisions and analyze privacy-related text. It also explores compliance gap analysis to support the review of privacy policies against applicable PDPL requirements.

## Main Components

- **Legal Information Retrieval (RAG):** Retrieves relevant legal provisions to support evidence-based answers to PDPL-related questions.
- **Privacy Policy Clause Classification:** Uses Machine Learning to classify privacy policy text into predefined categories.
- **Compliance Gap Analysis:** Supports the identification of potential gaps between privacy policy content and applicable compliance requirements.
